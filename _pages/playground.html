Website Agent

A small research agent running LFM2.5-2.6B in your browser. It plans its steps, asks clarifying questions, searches Wikipedia, runs Python for calculations, and can use your location if you allow it. You can watch its thinking and every tool call live.

  • ~1.5 GB one-time download (cached)
  • Needs WebGPU: desktop Chrome / Edge, or Safari 26+
  • Runs locally; Wikipedia and other tools are called directly from your browser
